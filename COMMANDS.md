# Commands

- `/shards [balance [player]]` — view a balance.
- `/shards pay <online-player> <amount>` — atomically transfer shards.
- `/shardshop` — open the Cosmic Confection Exchange.
- `/afk [list|join|leave|random|info]` — AFK-zone interface.
- `/shardmanager give|take|set <player> <amount>` — ledger administration.
- `/shardmanager reset|reload|history|rollback|zone|migrate|debug|version|gui` — reserved administrative surfaces.

Aliases for `/shards`: `/donutshards`, `/dshards`.
